2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimal information strictly necessary to provide and authenticate our software:

🔑 A. Google Account Information (OAuth 2.0 Sign-In)

When you log in to our web applications using Google OAuth ("Sign in with Google"), we collect your basic profile information:

  • Full Name: To personalize your user dashboard and profile.
  • Email Address: To authenticate your identity, secure your login session, and send critical transactional updates.
  • Google User ID: A secure unique identifier provided by Google to verify your identity across sessions.
  • Profile Photo (Optional): To display your avatar within the application interface.

📂 B. Google Drive Data (Syllabus Tracker)

Syllabus Tracker requests access to Google Drive (using Google Drive API scopes such as drive.file) strictly to:

  • Read & Open: Access syllabus files, curricula documents, and lesson plans that you explicitly create or select with the app.
  • Save & Manage: Save and organize syllabus spreadsheets, educational progress files, and documents directly within your connected Google Drive storage.
  • Sync Workflow: Keep your educational syllabus data in sync across your devices directly in your Google Drive.

⚠️ Notice: Syllabus Tracker does NOT scan, browse, index, or access any other personal files, folders, or photos in your Google Drive.

C. Technical & Log Information

Standard technical logs (browser type, IP address, device type, error logs) are collected strictly to maintain system uptime, prevent DDoS attacks, and diagnose bugs.
